Privacy Policy

Emoji Fighter 3D · Edge Kase Interactive · Last updated October 20, 2026

The short version: the Emoji Fighter apps for iPhone, iPad and Android collect no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, in-app purchases or tracking. The website version uses Google Analytics to count visits.

The iOS and Android apps

  • The apps do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.
  • The game runs entirely on your device and works offline. It sends nothing over the network.
  • Your settings (music, sound and vibration on or off) are saved only on your device, and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • The apps contain no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking SDKs.

The website (emoji-fighter web version)

When you play in a web browser, we use Google Analytics to understand how many people play and which features they use (for example, pages visited and events such as a fight starting or a stage being cleared). Google Analytics uses cookies and collects technical information such as your browser type, device type and approximate location derived from your IP address. We use this only in aggregate, to improve the game. You can learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Your settings in the web version are kept in your browser's local storage on your device.

Children

Emoji Fighter is suitable for a general audience. Because the apps collect no personal information, they collect none from children.
